# VANSH.TECH Hybrid Workplace Policy

## Overview

This document outlines the official hybrid workplace policy for VANSH.TECH employees, effective during the one-month pilot program (August 2026).

---

## Policy Summary

**Schedule:** 3 Office Days + 2 Remote Days per week  
**Duration:** One-month pilot program (Test → Monitor → Collect → Improve)  
**Status:** PILOT PROGRAM ACTIVE

---

## Policy Guidelines

### 📌 Office Day Policy

The three office days are reserved for:

1. **Important Meetings** - Team syncs, client calls, leadership updates
2. **Teamwork** - Collaborative problem-solving and project work
3. **Brainstorming** - Creative sessions and idea generation
4. **Training** - In-person learning and skill development
5. **Relationship Building** - Face-to-face team bonding

### 💻 Remote Day Policy

The two remote days focus on:

1. **Individual Work** - Personal task execution and deliverables
2. **Research** - Deep investigation and learning
3. **Documentation** - Writing, reporting, knowledge management
4. **Concentration** - Tasks requiring undisturbed focus
5. **Flexible Scheduling** - Better work-life balance

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I adjust my remote/office days?**  
A: The pilot schedule is fixed for consistency. Adjustments may be considered based on pilot results.

**Q: What if I need to work from home on an office day?**  
A: Notify your manager as soon as possible with a valid reason. Approval may be granted on a case-by-case basis.

**Q: Will my performance be tracked differently?**  
A: No. Performance expectations remain the same. We measure results, not location.

**Q: Can teams have different schedules?**  
A: During pilot, all teams follow the same schedule for consistency. Future variations may be considered.

**Q: What's the status after one month?**  
A: Based on collected feedback, we'll decide to: Continue, Modify, or Discontinue the hybrid model.
